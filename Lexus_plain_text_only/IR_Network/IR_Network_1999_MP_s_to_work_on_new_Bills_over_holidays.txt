MP's to work on new Bills over holidays

Reported on: 17 December 1999

Members of Parliament's justice and constitutional development portfolio committee will work throughout the holidays to ensure­that they complete crucial pieces of legislation ­ including the complex Open Democracy Bill ­ in time for the February 4 constitutional deadline. 

They were instructed by chairman Mr Johnny De Lange to work from their homes over this festive season so that they could finalise their input on the Open Democracy, and the administrative justice legislation. 

The justice committee has one of the toughest schedules of all the committees trying to meet the February 4 deadline ­ compared to the committee considering the Promotion of Equality and Prevention of Unfair Discrimination Bill, also known as the Equality Bill, and the committee considering the Preferential Procurement Policy Framework Bill ­ the law that seeks to regulate how government tenders are awarded. 

Members would have to spend the majority of their time on revising the Open Democracy Bill. 

The Bill gives effect to an individual's constitutional right of access to any information held by the state; to provide people with access to personal information held by private bodies and to provide for the correction of personal information held by state or private bodies". 

The Bill seeks to ensure the right of privacy with regulations on the "use and disclosure of that information". The section on the protection of whistle blowers in the Bill aims to "provide for the protection of persons disclosing evidence of contravention's of the law, serious maladministration or corruption in governmental bodies". 

One of the issues MP's will grapple with is whether to insert a special privatisation clause in the Bill that will protect the information of state parastatals such as Telkom. 

De Lange argued that this clause will prevent potential rivals from gaining an unfair advantage if companies like Telkom are compelled to reveal information about the privatisation process.
