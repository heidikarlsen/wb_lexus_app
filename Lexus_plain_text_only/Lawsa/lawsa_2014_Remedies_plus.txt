



23 Remedies
The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)


LAWSA  >  The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)  >  Labour Law: Part 2  >  Resolution Of Labour Disputes  >  Unfair Labour Practices  >  Specific Cases Of Unfair Labour Practices  >  Occupational Detriment Suffered Because Of A Protected Disclosure (Whistleblowing)

23  Remedies  The employee occupationally prejudiced by his or her protected disclosures is in terms of:
(a) the Protected Disclosures Act;1 or 
(b) the Labour Relations Act 2 on account of an unfair labour practice committed against him or her 3 entitled to:
(c) status quo relief if demoted;4
(ii) reinstatement;5 and/or
(iii) compensation,6 including solatium;7
(iv) an interdict to prevent disciplinary proceedings taken against him or her on account of disclosures made by him or her regarding improprieties.8
  If, however, he or she is dismissed on account of his or her protected disclosures, such dismissal would constitute an automatically unfair dismissal.9 The fact that the employee participated in the disciplinary enquiry, does not mean that he or she waived the protection afforded by PDA.10
