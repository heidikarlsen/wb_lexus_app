Labour court to hear whistleblower's redeployment case

Reported on 16 March 2015 

Solidarity is heading to the Labour Court on Monday over the redeployment of whistleblower Colonel Kobus Roos within the police.

The union said national police commissioner Riah Phiyega was expected to testify in the case on Monday.

However, it was not clear if Phiyega would be in court.

SA Police Service national spokesperson Solomon Makgale said if the commissioner was required to go to court she would go. He could not confirm if she would be present.

Roos, who has worked for the police for 26 years, was originally demoted by ex-crime intelligence boss Richard Mdluli, after he exposed alleged corruption in the crime intelligence unit.

Mdluli removed Roos from his position as head of internal audit and placed him in the inspectorate and evaluation division of the union -- a post the union said was redundant.

In April, the Labour Court ordered the police to redeploy Roos to a similar position as that he had before his demotion.

In the court ruling, the police were also ordered to pay Roos R156 250 in compensation and pay costs.

Solidarity has said that the police have not complied with the order, however Makgale was insistent that the SAPS had.
