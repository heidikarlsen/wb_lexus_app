



(ZA) Proc 199 of 11 October 2024:  Transfer of the administration and powers and functions entrusted by legislation to certain cabinet members in terms of section 97 of the Constitution ( (Government Gazette No. 51368)
South Africa National Subordinate Legislation


National Subordinate Legislation  >  ZA Subordinate Legislation  >  C  >  Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996  >  Rules and Regulations  >  Proclamations

by the

President of the Republic of South Africa

I hereby, in terms of section 97 of the Constitution of the Republic of South Africa, 1996, transfer—

(a) the administration and powers and functions of the legislation specified in Column 1 of Tables 1 to 6 in the Schedule from the Cabinet member specified in Column 2 of the said Tables, immediately before the President assumed office on 19 June 2024, to the Cabinet member specified in Column 3 of the said Tables; and

(b) the powers and functions exercised by the Cabinet member specified in Column 1 of Table 7 in the Schedule, immediately before the President assumed office on 19 June 2024, in terms of the legislation specified in Column 2 of Table 7 to the Cabinet member specified in Column 3 of Table 7.

Given under my Hand and the Seal of the Republic of South Africa at Pretoria this 01 day of October Two Thousand and Twenty Four.

(Signed)

President

By Order of the President-in-Cabinet:

(Signed)

Minister of the Cabinet
