The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)


LAWSA  >  The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)  >  Labour Law: Part 2  >  Annual Cumulative Supplement 2025  >  Resolution of Labour Disputes: Unfair Labour Practices
22   General protected disclosure

TEXT AND NOTES 2 4–7: S 9(1)–(3) has been amended by including in its ambit a “worker”: Protected Disclosures Amendment Act 5 of 2017 s 9(a)–(c).
  S 1 sv “worker” has been inserted to mean —
(a) any person who works or worked for another person or for the State; or
(b) any other person who in any manner assists or assisted in carrying on or conducting or conducted the business of an employer or client,

as an independent contractor, consultant, agent; or
(c) any person who renders services to a client while being employed by a temporary employment service: Protected Disclosures Amendment Act 5 of 2017 s 1(g).
  S 1 sv “temporary employment service” has been inserted to mean any person who, for reward, procures for or provides to a client other persons who —
(a) render services to, or perform work for, the client; and
(b) are remunerated by the temporary employment service: Protected Disclosures Amendment Act 5 of 2017 s 1(f).

NOTES 2–3: See John v Afrox Oxygen Ltd 2018 5 BLLR 476 (LAC); Chowan v Associated Motor Holdings (Pty) Ltd 2018 2 All SA 720 (GJ).

TEXT AND NEW NOTE 7A: S 9A has been inserted to provide for immunity against civil and criminal liability flowing from a disclosure of information which shows or tends to show that a criminal offence has been committed, is being committed or is reasonably likely to be committed: Protected Disclosures Amendment Act 5 of 2017 s 10.

TEXT AND NEW NOTE 7B: S 9B has been inserted to create an offence for the disclosure of false information: Protected Disclosures Amendment Act 5 of 2017 s 10.
