239   Pre-dismissal inquiry

NOTE 3: Disciplinary proceedings conducted under section 188A of the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995 are conducted by agreement. It is inconceivable that an employee should agree to that process and then claim that it amounted to an unfair labour practice: Department of International Relations and Cooperation v Laubscher 2023 1 BLLR 1 (LAC).

NEW NOTE 8A: When the Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995 was amended in 2015, the heading of section 188A had been changed from a “pre-dismissal inquiry” to “inquiry by arbitrator”. The amended section provided for an arbitrator to inquire into allegations against employees relating to their conduct or capacity. However, an exception was made in section 188A(11), which allows either the employer or the employee to refer a dispute involving allegations of alleged protected disclosures. Although the Rules for Conduct of Proceedings before the Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) rule 34 had been amended to cater for the new section 188A(11), a referral form does not amount to a formal pleading and commissioners are not bound by them. Protected disclosures need not be accurate, but they must be made in good faith: Pedlar v Performing Arts Council of the Free State (PACOFS) 2021 6 BALR 649 (CCMA).
