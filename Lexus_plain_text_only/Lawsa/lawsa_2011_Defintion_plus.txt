The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)


LAWSA  >  The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)  >  Labour Law: Part 2  >  Resolution Of Labour Disputes  >  Unfair Labour Practices  >  Unfair Labour Practices In Terms Of The Labour Relations Act

9  Definition  The Labour Relations Act 1 defines “unfair labour practice” as any unfair act or omission that arises between an employer and an employee, involving:
(a) the unfair conduct of the employer relating to the promotion, demotion, probation2 (excluding disputes relating to probation dismissals) or training of an employee or relating to the provision of benefits to an employee;3
(b) the unfair suspension of an employee4 or any other disciplinary action short of dismissal in respect of an employee;5 
(c) the failure or refusal by an employer to reinstate or re-employ a former employee in terms of any agreement;6 or
(d) an occupational detriment, short of dismissal, wrongfully7 suffered by an employee, on account of having made a protected disclosure.8
  From this definition flow a couple of general requirements (elements) which should be complied with, namely:
(a) any act or omission constituting a labour practice;9
(b) protection is only afforded to employees;10
(c) against any unfair11 act or omission;12
(d) during duration of employment relationship.13
