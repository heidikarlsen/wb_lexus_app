



143 Right to job and subsistence security
The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)


LAWSA  >  The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)  >  Labour Law: Part 1  >  Individual Labour Law  >  Duties Of Employers  >  Duties In Terms Of Labour Legislation

143  Right to job and subsistence security1  The constitutional right to fair labour practices implies that there is a duty on an employer to provide job and subsistence security to his or her employees. This means that every employee has the right:
(a) to render his or her services in a safe working environment with safe machinery and equipment;2
(b) that his or her life and health will not be exposed to unnecessary dangers;3
(c) to be compensated in cases of job injuries4 or unemployment;5
(d) not to be molested sexually6 or otherwise harassed (prejudiced) at work;7 
(e) to make protected disclosures freely without being prejudiced by his or her employer;8 and
  (f )   not to be dismissed unfairly on grounds of alleged misconduct,9 incompetence,10 discrimination,11 retrenchment,12 and so forth.13
