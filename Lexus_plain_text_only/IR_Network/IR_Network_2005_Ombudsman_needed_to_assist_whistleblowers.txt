Ombudsman needed to assist whistleblowers 

Reported on: 06 December 2005

An ombudsman should be set up to deal with and assist whistleblowers, Parliament heard today. Addressing the ad-hoc committee on governance, the Open Democracy Advice Centre (ODAC) said the courts cannot be the only place to deal with this issue. 

Whistle blowing and highlighting corruption have become big issues for companies, as well as for government. But in doing so, whistleblowers are often isolated and pay the price for speaking out. 

Alison Tilley of ODAC told the committee that there is no protection in place for people who expose corruption and instead of going to the courts they could go to the ombudsman. 

The input of this and other bodies will form part of the governance committee's report into Parliament's submission to the African Peer Review Mechanism. Parliament's input into the final report will be unedited and completed by March next year.
