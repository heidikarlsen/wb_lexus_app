The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)


LAWSA  >  The Law of South Africa (LAWSA)  >  Energy  >  Liquid Fuels  >  Mineral And Petroleum Resources Development Act  >  General And Miscellaneous Provisions

115  Prohibitions  No person may obstruct, hinder or oppose any authorised person in the performance of his or her duties or the exercise of his or her powers or functions in terms of the Mineral and Petroleum Resources Development Act.1
  The holder of a right, permit or permission may not subject his or her employees to any occupational detriment on account of such employees disclosing information to the minister or director-general or other authorised person, regarding the failure by such holder to comply with any provision of the Act, or to the effect that such holder is conducting an exploration or production operation in contravention of the objects of the Act and the social and labour plan.2 Occupational detriment means “occupational detriment” as defined in the Protected Disclosures Act.3


Footnotes
1	28 of 2002 s 94. See par 112 ante on “authorised person”.
2	S 95(1).
3	26 of 2000.
